Lowkey Yall Sleeping on Old Demo Music (SCHWANK MUSIC YAPPENING #1)

By: Schwank

Ok so... we've gotta talk about rompler/synth music today #hellyes. I've been obsessed with this shit for the past year or so and I'm honestly so suprised that more people aren't tapped in since there's a lot of cool history involving some p cool tools. Uh I guess before we start I can dive into my personal fav trinkets and doodads

KORG GLAZE
First up is defo the korg triton, should say I'll be going off the vst versions for a lot of these because I'm a college student, but the korg plugins I find have some of the best sounds when compared to the hardware counterparts. The triton is loaded with a ton of classic presets, I'm personally a big fan of the pianos bc they kinda are m1 coded but like in a different way, there's this one 80s epiano preset that i love to death on there. Also a lot of the evolving/pad sounds on there are diabolical as well, even though this is a rompler glazing post i gotta say like damn the korg wavestation.... there's not much more to say than that LMAO, I'm such an avid fan of it, has such a wonderful tonality. Originally got into it because i saw the mac start up sound was made with it and wanted to dive into it for my desktop pet alias and like... yeah its done wonders. but triton, m1, and wavestation all really fucking good vst couterparts. IMO must grabs if you're going for that late 90s->early 2000s sound since so many of those sounds come from these.

XV5080/JV1080
Ok now those are out of the way... gotta glaze the xv5080, the vst is like... nowhere near as good but still workable if you're ok with processing it out a bit, i find a good compressor/saturator on certain freq ranges (mainly mids/highs) does some wonders, def needs some reverb work as well imo, but the sounds are def there. I'm a sucker for the pan flute and this thing def has my favorite pan flute preset. it hits yo.... Otherwise the drums have a SOUND to them, i use them a ton personally (ex. freaky bug, ttmab breakdowns) and I think they really give a cool texture to whatever you throw em on. The other sounds are really good on the 5080 also, some other highlights imo are synth brass and strings, I feel like the korg ones are really good for having a high quality sound but I really dig the charm that the 5080 and 1080 have. A lot of people always talk about the 1080 also and like... yeah its a fucking beast but idk why I've been partial to the 5080, has more of a digital sound to it which I actually kind of like oddly enough.

EMULATORX
EmulatorX is another one i'd like to mention, because it is an AWFUL experience to get working. Since it only comes in VST2, you'll have to run with bridging software if ur trying to use modern fl like me and like 90% of the time it like... doesnt work at all and crashes. BUT... I've barely seen anyone talk about these sounds and they've def got like a alt. universe midi quality to them that I REALLY like, it almost makes the suffering worth it

I've been a big fan of universe's arrangements since I started diving into the wonderful world of romplers, they cover a ton of yosuke yasui stuff w/ various romplers. I'm a really big fan of their xv5080 usage, I feel like their arrangements feel like the logical step in evolution (obvs fm synths are like super goat supreme status) but the fm leads from fm8/dx7 play so well with the 5080s sound set. It also shows that even though the 5080 is an older synth it plays really well with more modern tools as you can see in the description a few more familiar tools were used like kontakt and battery. but yeah I'm a huge fan, check out their other stuff also if you aren't sold on rompler stuff, it all just has this charm im obsessed w/

Not a rompler but damn can we talk about the JD-800, the bass sounds specifically on this thing are always so punchy and I adore them to death, you can really hear it on this demo specifically. Reminds me of like a more polished dx7 bass if that makes sense idk, this demo is pretty chill though I can get jiggy to it personally. The drums on this one are also quite nice, im a big fan of the attack on a lot of these presets its really pleasant. This is also the funny mario dire dire docks synth so if thats your vibe hell yes. I'm an avid fan of a lot of the bell/atmos elements on this thing. The pianos are also quite nice which u can hear throughout these demos, it has a very midi like quality (probably because midi libraries sample roland stuff lol) but they're really clean i fw them heavy.

Imo the integra is the like rompler final boss, this thing is everything I want in a rompler, great raw sounds that cover a large basis of genres. They all are incredibly clean and have a wonderful charm to them. Roland I stg please kill zenology and make an integra vst please im fucking begging it would fix me yo. All the demos are crazy but daybreak is probably my favorite because you can just hear how expressive some of these sounds can get, I'm a big fan of how the muted brass was played specifically and that SAX.... MMMMMMM perfection. Just all incredible sounds though i hope to own one of these physically one day :3. Also yeah the composition for daybreak is so good also, the trading between which instrument takes the lead role is so nice and that chord prog is so mysterious mgh.m .. so fucking good yo

Anyways on the topic of missing old synths can we bring back 80s/90s contemporary composition pleassseee ty :3333. I've been really obsessed w/ James Ingram discography recently, even though lyrically a lot of these songs are simple love songs or similar they all hit a lot harder imo because of the incredible composition/jazz influence. His duets have to be some of my favorites personally, there's always a keychange or two that hits like a TRUCK and ugh,. im just obsessed with it yo. A lot of these songs also use rhodes and era specific epianos duoed with either a rompler or phyiscal piano and I think that combo just works so well in context. Recordings from this era also have such a... grain? to them i geuss that i really love, especially on strings, they have a very melancholic tone ig thats kind of lost in newer recordings I feel. This is one of my personal favorite James songs, the chorus on this one is pretty diabolical imo, has a very yerny quality to it which I really love! for this post I actually did a little analysis of it and I'm a huge fan on how the song trades between f min and Ab maj for verse and chorus. Makes it feel like the one side is the storm and the other is the light at the end of the tunnel, although there are storms hiccups in the relationship in the end I'll be there, that wont change, and we'll have to make our own sun is how I interpret it. It seems like a basic thing to say but I am a big fan of how these older ballads can stand without lyrics, I feel like they only strengthen the message thats in the song, I feel like joe reddit edm #1405 but in a lot of electronic music i think that's missing especially in the more modern landscape. Not to say there arent great examples of this either and not every song has to be like super moody either (ex: poplife by underscores/umru does what its trying to do perfectly and I adore it, the lyrics only add to the goal i feel). The V/V in the second repition also packs a punch. But yeah this section wouldn't hit nearly as hard w/o prior context, without the keychange i feel this would feel pretty standard.
